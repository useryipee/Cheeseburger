Privacy policy

This policy explains what Cheeseburger may access and how that information is used when the bot is active in a Discord server.

Messages and message content

Cheeseburger may read messages sent in channels where it has access. Message content may be processed and stored to build its Markov memory, generate responses and operate bot features. Messages may also be used to identify commands and provide game or economy functions.

Display names and user IDs

Cheeseburger can access your Discord display name and unique Discord user ID. These details may be used to identify users, associate saved balances and game progress with the correct account, and display names in bot responses or leaderboards.

Online status and activity presence

When the bot is configured with Discord's Presence Intent and has access to presence data, it may observe whether members are online, idle, do not disturb or offline. Presence information may be used by bot features. The bot does not have access to your private Discord activity or account credentials.

Storage and retention

Some bot data, such as Markov memory, balances, portfolios and game progress, may be stored in local data files maintained by the bot operator. Data may remain stored until the operator deletes or resets those files. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information to the bot.

Sharing

Cheeseburger is intended to use this information to operate its features. The bot operator should not publish private message content or share stored user data with others except where necessary to operate the bot, comply with law, or address abuse and security issues.

Your choices

You can avoid sending messages in channels where Cheeseburger operates, and you can leave the server if you do not want to interact with the bot. For questions or requests about stored bot data, contact the server or bot operator.

Operator note: This policy should accurately reflect the bot's real settings and code. In particular, presence tracking only works if the Discord Presence Intent is enabled and the bot actually reads or stores presence information. Update this page if those practices change.
